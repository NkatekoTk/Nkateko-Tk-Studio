TK DIGITAL STUDIO — WEBSITE

1. Open index.html in Chrome/Edge to preview the website.
2. Replace the placeholder WhatsApp number in script.js:
   const WHATSAPP_NUMBER = "27000000000";
   Use your South African number in international format, without + or spaces.
3. Replace the Instagram/TikTok placeholder links in index.html.
4. Replace hello@tkdigitalstudio.co.za with your real email if you want.
5. The current site is a front-end store: orders are sent to WhatsApp.
6. Do NOT put card details or bank credentials into the website code.
7. For online payments, connect a verified payment provider such as Payfast by Network after setting up the merchant account. A real payment integration requires merchant credentials and server-side/security configuration.

FILES
- index.html: website structure/content
- style.css: visual design and responsive layout
- script.js: product catalogue, filtering and WhatsApp orders
